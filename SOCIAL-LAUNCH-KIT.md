# Backroads Pizza & Bourbon Bar — Social Launch Kit

- Instagram: `@backroadspizza`
- Facebook: `Backroads Pizza & Bourbon Bar`

Check availability before reserving each handle. Keep the same avatar, name, bio, and link across all three.

## Bio

Pizza. Bourbon. NFL Sundays.\
Highfill, Arkansas · Opening 2027\
Come a stranger. Leave a friend.

## Content pillars

1. **The road to opening** — construction, the garage doors, bourbon-wall progress, and the people building it.
2. **Pizza trials** — dough, oven testing, first-slice reactions, and signature pie reveals.
3. **The neighborhood table** — Highfill stories, local events, staff introductions, and nearby partners.
4. **Sunday at Backroads** — game-day food, bourbon picks, and the sports-bar atmosphere.

## First six posts

1. **The announcement** — “Highfill, we’re taking the long way home. Backroads Pizza & Bourbon Bar is opening in 2027.”
2. **The promise** — The brand story and “Come a stranger. Leave a friend.”
3. **The place** — Map/location post: NW 67th Ave & Hwy 12.
4. **The pizza** — Introduce The Highfill: pepperoni, cup & char, hot honey, mozzarella.
5. **The bar** — A tease of the floor-to-ceiling bourbon wall.
6. **The countdown** — Invite followers to sign up for opening-day news.

## Voice

Warm, confident, plainspoken, and local. It should sound like the person who saves you a stool—not a restaurant marketing calendar.
