Our story · Draft copy

Come a stranger.
Leave a friend.

A place taking shape in Highfill, Arkansas. Final narrative copy is still to come.

Backroads is being built as a pizza-forward neighborhood bar with Italian influence and a serious bourbon program. It’s a place for a weeknight pie, a table after the game, and the familiar faces you’ll see again the next Sunday.

With about 45 seats inside, 16 on the patio, garage doors to the evening, and a 28-foot bar at the heart of the room, we’re making space for the kind of night that feels easy from the start.

Some places just feel like home. Good people. Cold drinks. Long nights. The kind of place that keeps you a little longer.
